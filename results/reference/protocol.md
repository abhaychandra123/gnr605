# October 2022 reference experiment

This small exploratory experiment compares the assignment's NDVI > 0.30 rule with a supervised Landsat classifier in Beed. October 2022 has 88.9% valid Landsat coverage. Reference evidence is interpreted from a separate sensor (Sentinel-2). Field observations would provide stronger evidence.

Before looking at the chips or evaluating either method, fix the following choices. Sample 32 western training and 32 eastern test sites, half on each side of the baseline threshold to include difficult examples. Use a 12 km east–west gap and at least 900 m between sites. Assign shuffled opaque identifiers. Display only Sentinel-2 RGB and infrared false colour with a central footprint guide. Use the closest clear October Sentinel-2 observation within three days of an accepted Landsat date. Record green vegetation when most of the central Landsat footprint visibly contains live vegetation, other for predominantly water, built or exposed ground, and uncertain for ambiguous mixtures or obscuration. Exclude uncertain and unavailable references and report their counts. One AI interpreter supplies the image judgements before training or viewing model scores. The sampling design enriches below-threshold sites and the results apply to this sample.

Use red, green, NIR and both SWIR reflectances (B3–B7). Standardize using training sites alone. Predict with the majority among the three nearest training sites by squared Euclidean distance. Compare its predictions with the existing monthly NDVI baseline on identical eastern sites. Record the confusion matrices, class recalls and balanced accuracy. The experiment measures agreement with image interpretation. Temporal mismatch with monthly mean Landsat, sample composition, single interpretation and one geographic holdout constrain the inference.
